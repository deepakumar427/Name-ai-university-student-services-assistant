SAMPLE UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY
ACADEMIC REGULATIONS FOR B.TECH PROGRAMMES (VERSION 3.1)
Issued by the Office of the Dean (Academics). Effective from 1 July 2024.
SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT CREATED FOR THE HACKATHON. NOT AN OFFICIAL DOCUMENT.

1. Short title and commencement
1.1 These regulations are called the Academic Regulations for B.Tech Programmes, 2024. They apply to all B.Tech students from the academic year 2024-25 onwards.

2. Definitions
2.1 "Course" means a subject with a course code, for example CS201 Data Structures.
2.2 "End-semester examination" means the examination held at the end of each semester for every course.
2.3 "Active backlog" means a course in which a student has failed or was absent and has not yet passed.

5. Evaluation
5.1 Marks distribution. Each course is evaluated out of 100 marks: internal assessment carries 40 marks and the end-semester examination carries 60 marks.
5.2 Pass mark. A student must obtain at least 40 marks out of 100 in total to pass a course.
5.3 Result. The result of a course is declared as PASS, FAIL, ABSENT or DETAINED.
<<<PAGE>>>
7. Attendance
7.1 Counting attendance. Attendance is counted separately for each course as the number of classes attended divided by the number of classes held.
7.2 Minimum attendance. A student must have a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to appear in the end-semester examination of that course.
7.3 Condonation. The Dean (Academics) may condone a shortage of attendance of up to 5% on documented medical grounds, on written application by the student.
7.4 Detention. A student who does not meet the minimum attendance requirement, after any condonation, is detained in that course and is not allowed to appear in its end-semester examination.

9. Supplementary examinations
9.1 Eligibility. A student who has failed, or was absent in, the regular end-semester examination of a course is eligible to appear in the supplementary examination of that course. A student who was detained in a course is not eligible for its supplementary examination.
9.2 Procedure. To appear in a supplementary examination a student must: (a) register for the supplementary examination on the examination portal within 10 days of the declaration of results; (b) pay the supplementary examination fee notified by the Controller of Examinations; (c) download the admit card from the examination portal; and (d) appear in the examination as per the published schedule.
9.3 Marks. The marks obtained in the supplementary examination replace the earlier end-semester examination marks of that course.

10. Backlogs
10.1 A course is counted as an active backlog until the student passes it in a regular or supplementary examination.
