SAMPLE UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY
OFFICE OF THE DEAN (ACADEMICS)
CIRCULAR No. ACAD-2026-08. Dated 20 July 2026.
SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT CREATED FOR THE HACKATHON. NOT AN OFFICIAL DOCUMENT.

1. Subject
1.1 Revision of the minimum attendance requirement for end-semester examinations.

2. Revised minimum attendance
2.1 With effect from 1 August 2026, a student must have a minimum of 80% attendance in each course to be eligible to appear in the end-semester examination of that course.
2.2 This circular supersedes clause 7.2 of the Academic Regulations for B.Tech Programmes (version 3.1).

3. Other provisions
3.1 Condonation under clause 7.3 of the Academic Regulations continues to apply unchanged.
