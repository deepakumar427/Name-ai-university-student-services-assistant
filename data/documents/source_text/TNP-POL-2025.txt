1. Purpose
1.1 This policy sets out who may register for campus placement and how.

3. Eligibility for campus placement
3.1 Minimum CGPA. A student must have a CGPA of at least 6.5 to register for campus placement.
3.2 Backlogs. A student must have no active backlogs at the time of registration for campus placement.
3.3 A student who clears all backlogs later in the year may register in the next placement drive.

4. Registration procedure
4.1 Eligible students must register on the placement portal, upload an updated resume, and confirm their details with the Training and Placement Cell.
