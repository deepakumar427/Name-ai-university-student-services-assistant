Q1. How do I get a bonafide certificate?
Apply at the Student Section counter with your ID card. Certificates are issued within 3 working days.

Q2. Where can I see the examination timetable?
The timetable is published on the examination portal and on the notice board of each department.

Q3. What attendance do I need to sit for the end-semester examinations?
Students need at least 65% attendance in a course to sit for its end-semester examination.

Q4. How do I apply for a hostel room?
Submit the hostel application form to the Chief Warden's office before the start of the semester.
